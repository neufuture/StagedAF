What you can do

Stage photos already in AppFolio

Every photo in a unit's Photos section gets a Stage button. Pick one, stage it, and swap it in without downloading or re-uploading anything.

Stage new uploads as you add them

Switch on “Stage new uploads” and each time you add photos you choose which ones to furnish. They are staged before they ever reach AppFolio.

Several photos at once

Tick a whole set of empty rooms, or queue up one photo after another. They are staged side by side while you carry on working.

Eight furnishing styles

Match the unit and the renter you are aiming for.

  • Modern
  • Scandinavian
  • Mid-century modern
  • Transitional
  • Modern farmhouse
  • Coastal
  • Industrial
  • Traditional

Your own instructions

Say what you want in plain words: “sectional facing the window, warm neutrals, keep the floor clear”. Or pick the room type and leave the rest to it.

Before and after, every time

Each result is shown beside the original before anything changes. Not right? Adjust the style or instructions and regenerate.

One-click replace

The staged photo is uploaded, the original is removed, and the unit's default photo stays the default. A copy of the original is saved to your computer first.

The room stays the room

Furniture is added; walls, floors, windows and fixtures are left as they are, so the photo still shows the real unit.

Disclosure built in

Optionally stamp “Virtually staged” on each photo to meet listing-site and advertising rules.
