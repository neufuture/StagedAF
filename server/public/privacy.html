What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to contact you about your account.
  • Your credit balance and a record of credits used and bought.
  • Photos you choose to stage, and any instructions you type for them. These are sent to our server only when you press Stage.

What we do with photos

A photo you stage is sent to our image provider (OpenAI) to produce the staged version, and the result is returned to your browser. We do not keep copies of your photos or the staged results on our servers. OpenAI handles the photo under its own API data policies.

The extension only acts on AppFolio pages you open, and only reads the photos you ask it to stage. It does not collect tenant, owner or financial information from AppFolio.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe tells us that a payment succeeded so we can add your credits.

Who we share data with

Only the services needed to run UnitStager: Cloudflare (hosting), Supabase (accounts), OpenAI (image staging) and Stripe (payments). We do not sell your data.
