Privacy

Last updated: November 22, 2026. UnitStager is created and operated by Neufuture, LLC (“Neufuture”, “we”, “us”). See also our Terms of Service.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in, to contact you about your account, and to send a few short follow-up emails about using UnitStager (for example when your free credits are unused or run out). Every follow-up email has an unsubscribe link; sign-in codes and receipts are sent only when you ask for them.
  • Your credit balance and purchase history: credits used and bought, and the amount and date of each payment. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.
  • Sign-in information: the one-time code we email you, and the sign-in session that keeps you signed in. The session is stored in your browser (in the extension, or on our website) and can be cleared by signing out.
  • Photos you choose to stage, and any instructions you type for them. These are sent to our server only when you press Stage.
  • Which property and unit each credit was used for, when you stage from AppFolio: the property and unit names and AppFolio’s numbers for them, as shown on the page. This lets you see your usage per property.

What we do with photos

A photo you stage is uploaded to our server and sent to our image provider (OpenAI) to produce the staged version. The photo you uploaded is deleted as soon as staging finishes. OpenAI handles it under its own API data policies.

The staged result is stored for 30 days so you can download it again from Recent stagings, including if you closed the page before it finished. After 30 days it is deleted automatically. You can delete any staged photo sooner yourself.

The extension only acts on AppFolio pages you open, and only reads the photos you ask it to stage. Besides those photos, it records only the name and number of the property and unit you staged them from. It does not collect tenant, owner or financial information from AppFolio.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe tells us that a payment succeeded so we can add your credits.

If you turn on auto top-up, Stripe keeps your card on file so that we can charge it for the pack you chose when your credits run low. You can turn auto top-up off at any time in the extension's settings, and we stop charging the card.

Who we share data with

Only the services needed to run UnitStager: Cloudflare (hosting and storage of staged photos), Supabase (accounts), OpenAI (image staging) and Stripe (payments). We do not sell your data.
